# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in MaxOps — issues, pull requests, discussions — to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy towards other contributors, especially newcomers

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a GitHub issue, or, for sensitive reports, via [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/maxopsdev/maxops/security/advisories/new) as a stand-in private channel. All reports will be reviewed and result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
